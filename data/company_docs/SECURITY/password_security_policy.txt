NovaTech Solutions
Password and Account Security Policy

Document ID: SEC-PASS-001
Department: Information Security
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. Purpose

This policy defines the minimum password and account security requirements for employees of NovaTech Solutions.

2. Password Requirements

Employees must create passwords that are at least 12 characters long.

Passwords should contain a combination of uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters.

3. Password Sharing

Employees must never share their passwords with another employee, manager, IT support person, or external party.

IT support staff will never ask an employee to disclose their password.

4. Password Reuse

Employees should not reuse their company password for personal websites or external services.

5. Multi-Factor Authentication

Employees must use the company's approved multi-factor authentication method for systems that require it.

6. Suspected Account Compromise

If an employee believes that their account or password has been compromised, they should immediately contact the IT support team and reset the password.

7. Security Questions

Employees should contact the Information Security team for questions about password requirements or account security.
