VPN Troubleshooting Guide

Document ID: IT-VPN-001
Department: Information Technology
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. Purpose

This guide provides basic troubleshooting steps for employees who cannot connect to the company VPN.

2. Check Internet Connection

Before troubleshooting the VPN, verify that the device has a stable internet connection.

3. Restart the VPN Client

Close the VPN application completely and start it again.

4. Verify Credentials

Make sure the employee is using the correct company username and password.

5. Check Multi-Factor Authentication

Confirm that the required multi-factor authentication request has been approved.

6. Retry the Connection

After completing the previous steps, try connecting to the company VPN again.

7. Create an IT Support Ticket

If the VPN connection still fails after completing the troubleshooting steps, create an IT support ticket with the following information:

- Employee name
- Device name
- Operating system
- Error message
- Approximate time the problem started

8. Priority

A VPN issue affecting a single employee should normally be treated as a standard support request.

A VPN outage affecting multiple employees may require higher priority and escalation to the IT support team.
