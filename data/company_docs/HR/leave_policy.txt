NovaTech Solutions
Employee Leave Policy

Document ID: HR-LEAVE-001
Department: Human Resources
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. Purpose

This policy defines the leave rules applicable to employees of NovaTech Solutions.

2. Casual Leave

Full-time employees are entitled to 12 days of casual leave per calendar year.

Casual leave may be taken for personal requirements or short-term unforeseen situations.

Employees should submit a leave request through the company leave system whenever possible.

3. Sick Leave

Employees are entitled to 10 days of sick leave per calendar year.

For sick leave of more than two consecutive working days, the employee may be required to provide a medical certificate to Human Resources.

4. Annual Leave

Full-time employees are entitled to 18 days of annual leave per calendar year.

Annual leave should normally be requested at least three working days before the intended leave date.

5. Leave Approval

Leave requests are subject to manager approval and business requirements.

Submitting a leave request does not automatically mean that the leave has been approved.

6. Questions

For questions regarding leave balances or special leave situations, employees should contact the Human Resources department.
